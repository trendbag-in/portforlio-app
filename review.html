Stitch output review

Studio Editorial, screen by screen

Seventeen screens generated in Google Stitch against the TrendBag marketing spec, rendered at 1440px and reviewed against DESIGN.md. Four were regenerated after the design system was rebuilt from the spec file. Two carry blockers that must not ship.

Blocker — fabricated endorsements

Stitch invented a Trusted by row naming Vogue, Prada, Acne Studios, SSENSE, Gucci and Celine, and dressed the v2 hero backdrop in a Vogue cover. None of these are TrendBag partners. Shipping either would be a false endorsement claim using real companies' marks.

The four proof-band statistics are placeholders I wrote into the prompt, not real numbers. Both need real data or the band gets cut.

What happens next

Getting this into the React app

  1. Strip the invented brands and stats. Replace the logo row with real partners or cut the proof band entirely.

  2. Rebuild the hero from v1's composition with v2's nav and footer, and licensed photography of our own.

  3. Wire studio-editorial.css into the app — tokens, editorial devices and the motion layer are already written.

  4. Port section by section, starting with Who it's for and Features, which need the least correction.

  5. Add the motion layer in React. Stitch shipped almost none — only the map pin pulses. Scroll reveal, card lift, rule wipe and photo settle all get built here, behind a reduced-motion guard.

  6. Regenerate the photography with Indian models, and drop the placeholder product cutouts.
