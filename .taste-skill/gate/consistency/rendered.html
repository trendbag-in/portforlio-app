How to start as a UGC creator in India on TrendBag

Six steps from sign-up to your first payout. No agency, no follower threshold.

  1. 01Sign up

    Download the TrendBag app on Android or iOS, or open app.trendbag.in, and create your creator account.

  2. 02Build your profile with 2 samples

    Add a short bio, your style and two sample videos. Your profile is your portfolio, and it is what brands see when they review applications.

  3. 03Take the UGC basics lesson

    A free 15-minute lesson covers hooks, lighting and framing. Complete the test brief after it to earn a verified badge on your profile.

  4. 04Apply to briefs

    Browse briefs from fashion brands and apply to the ones that fit you. Brands can also invite you directly.

  5. 05Shoot and submit

    If the brief includes product, it is shipped to you. Shoot the video, submit it in the app, and handle up to 2 rounds of revisions if the brand asks.

  6. 06Get paid

    You are paid in ₹ for each approved video. Withdraw to your verified Indian bank account once your balance reaches ₹100.

Simple rules that keep your videos safe to publish and your payouts clean.

    Use your own voice or royalty-free tracks only, so the video can be posted and boosted without copyright claims.

Questions about TrendBag for creators

How to become a UGC creator in India?

To become a UGC creator in India, start by making a few short sample videos that show you can present a product clearly: a try-on, an unboxing or a GRWM. Then join a platform where brands post paid briefs. On TrendBag you sign up free, add two samples to your profile, take a 15-minute UGC basics lesson, and apply to fashion briefs. You do not need a large following to get started.

How much should UGC creators charge in India?

It depends on the deliverable, the effort involved and the usage rights the brand wants. A simple try-on Reel for organic posting usually pays less than a scripted video licensed for paid ads. On TrendBag, the brand sets the fee per brief, so you see it before you apply. As an example only, not a promise, fees could fall somewhere around ₹300–₹2,000 per video.

Do I need followers to become a UGC creator?

No. UGC is content that brands use on their own pages and ads, so they pay for the video rather than your audience. TrendBag has no follower minimum for UGC jobs. What matters is your profile: two strong samples, clear audio, good lighting and a verified badge from the UGC basics test brief help brands pick you.

More questions about TrendBag for creators

How do UGC creators get paid on TrendBag?

Each brief is funded by the brand upfront. When you submit a video, the brand can approve it or ask for up to 2 rounds of revisions. Once approved, the fee is added to your balance in ₹. If the brand does not respond within 72 hours, the video is auto-approved. You can withdraw to a verified Indian bank account from ₹100.

Who owns the UGC videos I make for brands?

You keep ownership of your videos. The brand gets a licence to use each video as described in the brief, for example on its own Instagram page or in paid ads for a set period. Check the usage rights in a brief before applying, because wider rights usually mean the brand should pay more for the video.

What is the difference between a UGC creator and an influencer?

An influencer is paid mainly for reaching their own audience. A UGC creator is paid to make a video that the brand uses, often on its own account or in ads. That is why UGC creators do not need a big following. Many creators do both, and on TrendBag a video can also be posted by you with the brand as collaborator.
