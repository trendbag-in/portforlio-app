Bodoni pulled back to the hero

A didone is a display face. It earns its keep at 60px and up, and becomes work to read below that — which is what happened when it was also setting every section heading. It now sets one thing, and everything you actually read is Karla.

Bodoni on the hero only · Karla everywhere else

The technical fix worth knowing

  • Optical size was working against us. Bodoni Moda is a variable font with an opsz axis, and browsers apply it automatically — as the type gets bigger, opsz climbs and the hairlines get thinner. The strokes were thinning exactly where the type was largest.
  • It is now pinned to opsz 16 with optical sizing off, so the hero keeps sturdy strokes at 78px instead of rendering as wire.
  • Dark mode still carries it to weight 800, since light-on-dark thins strokes optically on top of everything else.

If it is still not right

  • Fraunces — Option B from the specimens. Serif, warm, editorial, and far more robust than a didone because the contrast is much lower. Reads easily at any size.
  • Karla throughout — drop the display serif entirely. Cleanest and most readable, but the page loses its fashion-magazine voice.
