Applied

Bodoni Moda and Karla, live on the site

Option A is in. Every heading, label, button and figure has been retyped, and the code mono is gone from the codebase entirely.

Hero

Bodoni display · italic accent · Karla small-caps labels

Features

Karla card titles · small-caps chips and numerals

What it replaced

Plus Jakarta Sans · JetBrains Mono

Rules now in the spec

  • Bodoni only at scale — h1, h2, prices and large numerals. Never under 20px, never a paragraph.
  • The italic carries emphasis. The accent word is italic and terracotta, so the page leans less on colour.
  • Karla sets every sentence and, at 0.18–0.22em uppercase, every label.
  • No monospace anywhere. Small figures take Karla, not Bodoni — a 12px didone numeral is too fine to read.

Worth watching

  • Bodoni's hairlines thin out on dark mode — check the dark canvas before launch.
  • Buttons are now uppercase small caps; longer labels need more width than the sans did.
  • Two Google Fonts families with an italic axis — heavier font payload than the previous pair.
  • The product app still uses Plus Jakarta Sans. That divergence is deliberate, but it is a divergence.
